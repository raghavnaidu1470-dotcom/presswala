# PressWala — Quick Guide for the Vendor
*How to use the app, step by step*

---

### 1. Log In
Open the app link on your phone. Enter your **Vendor Key** (or your phone number) and your **4-digit PIN**. Tap **Sign In**.

---

### 2. See New Orders
Your Dashboard shows how many **New Orders** are waiting. Tap on **New Orders** (or **Manage Orders**) to see the flat number and the list of clothes for each order.

---

### 3. Mark "In Progress"
When you pick up the clothes from a flat, open that order and change its status to **In Progress**. This lets the resident know you have their clothes.

---

### 4. Mark "Ready"
Once the ironing is done, change the order's status to **Ready**. This tells the resident their clothes are ready for delivery.

---

### 5. Deliver & Record Payment
When you hand the clothes back at the door, mark the order as **Delivered**. The app will ask you one question: was it **Paid (Cash/UPI)** or **Unpaid (Collect Later)**? Always answer this — it cannot be skipped, and it is how the app remembers who still owes you money.

---

### 6. Check Outstanding Payments
Anytime you want to see who still owes you money, open **Outstanding Payments** on your Dashboard. You can tap **Remind on WhatsApp** to send a message, or **Mark Paid** once you collect the money.

---

## Important Reminders

- **Always answer Paid or Unpaid at delivery.** Never leave this blank — this one step is the whole reason the app exists.
- **Update prices only if a price actually changes.** Go to **Price List** in your portal to change the rate for shirts, pants, sarees, etc.
- **Keep your PIN private.** Don't share your 4-digit PIN with anyone — it protects your dashboard and your price list.
